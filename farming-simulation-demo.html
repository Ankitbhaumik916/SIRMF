Game Features

🌱 Soil Moisture

Each farm tile tracks soil moisture (0-100%). Moisture decreases through evaporation and increases through irrigation and rain.

  • Dry: < 30% (Brown)
  • Healthy: 40-70% (Green)
  • Wet: > 75% (Dark Blue)

🚜 Crop Health

Crop health (0-100%) improves when moisture is optimal and decreases in extreme conditions. Visualized as a colored circle on each tile.

  • Increases in optimal range
  • Decreases if too dry or wet
  • Affects farm productivity

💧 Irrigation System

Automatically activates when soil moisture drops below 35% and deactivates above 70%. Manual control coming in future updates.

  • Auto-activation threshold: 35%
  • Auto-deactivation threshold: 70%
  • Adds 15 moisture units/sec when active

⛅ Weather System

Weather affects evaporation rates and moisture gains. Changes randomly every 5 seconds.

  • ☀️ Sunny: 12 units/sec loss
  • ☁️ Cloudy: 6 units/sec loss
  • 🌧️ Rainy: 2 units/sec loss + 8 gain

🗺️ Farm Layout

The simulation includes a house at the center, water tank at top-left, pathways, and surrounding farmland tiles.

  • Brown/Tan: House & Pathways
  • Blue: Water Tank
  • Variable: Farmland (based on moisture)

👨‍🌾 Farmer Control

Move the farmer character around the map to inspect different farm tiles and understand the simulation behavior.

  • Navigate using keys
  • Press E to inspect farms
  • View detailed farm information

Tips for Optimal Farming

Optimal Moisture Range

Keep soil moisture between 40-70% for maximum crop health. This is the sweet spot where crops thrive and grow best.

Weather Adaptation

Monitor the weather system. During sunny days, decrease irrigation frequency. During rain, reduce manual irrigation to save water.

Avoid Overwatering

Overwatered crops (moisture > 75%) suffer crop health loss. The auto-system helps, but monitor individual farms for anomalies.

Track Farm Statistics

Use the dashboard to monitor average moisture and farms needing irrigation. This helps identify irrigation schedule optimization.
